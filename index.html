ABOUT ME

A little about my journey.

Who I Am

My name is Shemsa. I am a passionate learner, web developer and tailor. I enjoy coding because it allows me to turn ideas into useful and creative digital experiences.

I also love tailoring because it gives me another way to express my creativity. For me, coding and tailoring are different skills, but both allow me to create something meaningful from an idea.

My journey has not always been easy, but I am grateful for the people who have encouraged, guided and supported me along the way.

To All My Mentors ❤️

Marion, Nadia, Sana, Alice and Fashionista Kaberebende — thank you from the bottom of my heart.

I know I am still learning and that I have a long way to go, but when I look back at where I started and where I am today, I realize that I did not get here alone.

Each of you has touched a different part of my journey with opportunities, knowledge, encouragement, guidance, patience and love. You have helped shape the person, developer and tailor I am becoming.

Thank you for believing in me and for being part of my story. I truly appreciate everything you have done for me. ❤️

With a grateful heart,
Shemsa
